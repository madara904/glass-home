# uninstalled: restore.sh or Settings > Applications turns any of them back on.

# experiments, nag about updates, or are dead demo stubs. Anything a person might
# use (apps, the screensaver, printing, TV-provider sign-in) is in user-apps.txt and
# is only removed when asked for. Alexa is in alexa.txt and is kept unless asked.

# Protection varies per build: anything that answers "Cannot disable a protected
# package" belongs in protected.txt instead.
